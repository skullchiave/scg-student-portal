Before you apply

These rules come from immigration, not from the school.

You may work 28 hours per week at most. During long school holidays the limit is 8 hours per day. You need a work permit (資格外活動許可) on your residence card. Some jobs are not allowed at all — bars, pachinko, and similar places.
Ask the office first. Bring the job advertisement. A teacher will check the hours and the type of work with you before you apply. It takes 5 minutes and it has saved students their visa.
